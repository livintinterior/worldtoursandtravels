About Worlds Tours and Travels

Ten years of dependable journeys, one trip at a time.

Our Story

Built in Tirupati, Trusted Across South India

Worlds Tours and Travels started with a single sedan and a simple promise: show up on time, drive safely, and treat every passenger like family. Today, that promise has grown into a fleet of over 100 cabs, tempo travellers and buses serving pilgrims, families, students, and corporates across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.

Whether it's an early-morning Tirumala darshan trip, a college bus for 40 students, or a multi-country flight and visa plan, our team designs the journey around you — not the other way around.
